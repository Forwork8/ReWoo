How it works

A structured assessment of your workflows, data, and tooling that ends in a prioritized, reasoned roadmap — including the honest recommendation to do nothing where nothing is warranted. How every engagement runs: a short call about your work, then a written plan — what gets built, the timeline, what you receive at each step, and the terms, all in one document. For technical evaluators

How this engagement actually runs.

The method behind the offer — what is measured, what “done” means, and what lands in your hands. Terms are finalized per engagement in the written plan.

Method

  • Discovery instrument. Structured interviews plus a systems-and-data review — observed workflows, not aspiration surveys.
  • Scoring model. Value, feasibility, data readiness and risk, scored against weights agreed with you before the audit starts.
  • Output shape. A ranked opportunity map, one or two quick-win specifications, and a no-go list with reasons.

Acceptance criteria

  • Traceability. Every recommendation traces to an observed workflow and states its assumptions.
  • Challenge session. The map is defended in a working session with your team before it is final.

You receive

  • Deliverables. Written report, walkthrough session, and the scoring model left with you for reuse.
  • Decision log. What was recommended, why, and with what confidence — recorded for later review.

FAQ

Asked about this engagement.

Why not just start building?

Because build budget spent on the wrong workflow is the most expensive AI mistake there is. The audit exists to make your first build the right one.

What if the answer is we are not ready?

Then that is the finding, written down with the specific gaps — data quality, access, process stability — and what would change it. That answer saves more money than any roadmap.

Do you pitch your own services in it?

Recommendations include build-vs-buy calls that may point at existing tools instead of us. You also get the first-build scope in a form any competent builder can execute.

What happens after I get in touch?
