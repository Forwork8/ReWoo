What is included

The engagement, end to end.

  1. Pre-workshop discovery on your operation and goals
  2. A capabilities briefing grounded in current, verifiable AI reality
  3. Facilitated mapping of your workflows against that reality
  4. Risk and governance discussion: data, privacy, and human oversight
  5. A first prioritized plan built with the room
  6. A written summary with the plan and open questions

For technical evaluators

How this engagement actually runs.

The method behind the offer — what is measured, what “done” means, and what lands in your hands. Terms are finalized per engagement in the written scope.

Method

  • Pre-work. Short interviews and a pre-read before the session — the room spends its time on decisions, not discovery theater.
  • Structured exercises. Opportunity ranking, constraint mapping and sequencing — facilitated to a conclusion, not a brainstorm.
  • Written output. A first plan with owners, sequencing and explicit no-gos, agreed in the room before anyone leaves.

Acceptance criteria

  • Acceptance. The plan is specific enough to start the first item without another meeting — that is the bar.
  • Decision log. Every choice recorded with its rationale and its dissent, so later reviews make sense.

You receive

  • Deliverables. Decision log, first plan, and scheduled follow-up checkpoints.
  • Honest scope. What the workshop deliberately does not decide — and which engagement makes that decision properly.

FAQ

Asked about this engagement.

Is this a demo day?

No. Demos are cheap theater for decision-making. The briefing is about verifiable capability boundaries — including the embarrassing limits — because those decide your plan.

Who should attend?

The people who can commit resources and the people who own the workflows. A room without both produces a plan nobody executes.

Will we leave with a roadmap?

You leave with a first plan the room built and a written summary. Turning it into a verified roadmap with data checks is the natural follow-on.
