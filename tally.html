The cost founders pay globally

You run money across multiple global & local rails.
Nobody built a single tool for that.

QuickBooks and Xero assume your business receives revenue from a single local bank feed. They were built for traditional corporate accounting. Not for modern global founders receiving Stripe payouts, Wise transfers, WhatsApp voice notes, and regional payment apps.

Founders and fast-growing SMEs juggle separate payment rails across currencies and reconcile them manually. That's 15+ hours a week matching receipts against bank statements, currency conversions, and spreadsheets — with no single answer to "what's our actual available cash right now?"

The pain isn't that bookkeeping is hard. It's that no existing tool was built for the multi-currency, multi-rail way modern global businesses actually operate.

How it works

Five steps.
One always-current ledger.

From raw input to reconciled transaction — without you manually entering anything.

A

Submit

Send input however is natural to you: a photo of a bKash statement, a screenshot of a Nagad SMS, a photo of your handwritten cash ledger, or a WhatsApp voice note describing the day's transactions. No app download. No special format.

↗ WhatsApp or web upload
B

Extract

OCR reads statement and SMS images. Whisper transcribes voice notes. An LLM structuring step produces a transaction candidate — amount, date, counterparty, rail — with a confidence score. Every extraction is explicit, never guessed silently.

↗ OCR + Whisper + LLM structuring
C

Reconcile or Queue

Above the confidence threshold → auto-added to the ledger. Below it → held in a short review queue for one-tap human confirmation. Tally never silently guesses. Money mistakes are expensive and erode trust fast.

↗ Conservative threshold (~90%)
D

Ask

Ask a plain question anytime: "how much did we make this week?" or "what's our runway?" The answer is computed live from the reconciled ledger — with an "as of" timestamp — never estimated from incomplete data.

↗ Natural language query · timestamped answers
E

Feedback loop

Every human correction in the review queue is logged as a golden-set candidate — the same accuracy flywheel built for ReWoo's Ledger, now extended to extraction accuracy. Tally gets better with every correction.

↗ Golden-set eval-gate · continuous improvement

Non-negotiable principles

The same ethics bar
as everything we build.

Financial data privacy is absolute. Tally holds itself to stricter standards than ReWoo's other products — because this is raw financial detail, not interaction counts.

Never fabricates a number

Low-confidence extractions go to the human review queue — not a guessed value silently entered into your ledger. If Tally isn't sure, it tells you.

Financial data privacy is absolute

One client's transaction data is never visible to, compared against, or used to benchmark another client. Stricter than our aggregate-only rule — this is raw financial detail.

Human-in-loop, always

Below the confidence threshold, a human confirms — every time. We err conservative on what gets auto-reconciled. Money mistakes are expensive and erode trust fast.

Multi-rail from day one

bKash, Nagad, cash, and bank — all four, from the start. A single-rail v1 doesn't solve the actual problem, which is reconciliation across rails.

Every number is timestamped

Every ledger entry and runway figure carries an "as of" timestamp. You always know what the number is based on — and when it was last updated.

Works over WhatsApp

No app download. No special format. Send a photo or voice note via WhatsApp — the way these owners already communicate, not how a Western tool assumes they will.
