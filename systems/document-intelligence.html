In plain words

Reads your piles of documents — invoices, applications, contracts — and pulls out exactly the fields you need, with every answer linked to the line it came from.

An easy way to picture itLike a tireless clerk who never misfiles: reads the document, fills the form, and staples a copy of the exact line as proof.

Extraction
Pulling specific fields — dates, amounts, names — out of documents.
Classification

Traceability

What it does

The system, feature by feature.

Typed field extraction

Schemas you define — vendor, totals, dates, line items — extracted into validated fields.

Tables and multi-page handling

Line items and multi-document packs assembled correctly.

Classification and routing

Documents sorted into your queues with reasons attached.

Cross-checking and confidence flags

Extraction checked against rules and itself; low confidence goes to a human, always.

Audit trail

Every output field links back to the exact source region it came from.

Batch and bulk modes

Backlogs processed in controlled runs with full logging.

For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written scope.

Architecture

  • Pipeline shape. Ingest, classification, schema-constrained extraction, validation rules, human exception queue, system-of-record write-back.
  • Schema discipline. Field schemas are versioned and validated; documents that do not fit become exceptions, never guesses.
  • Traceability. Every extracted value stores a pointer to the exact source span — audit is a click, not an archaeology dig.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Per-field accuracy. Precision and recall measured per field on a sampled, human-verified set of your real documents; thresholds agreed in scope.
  • Exception budget. The share of documents allowed to route to humans is measured and reported — the number that matters for the business case.

Operations

  • Drift dashboards. Volumes, accuracy drift and exception reasons per document type, monitored continuously.
  • Security. Least-privilege access, encryption in transit and at rest, retention rules per document class.
  • Failure behavior. Ambiguous extractions route to the exception queue with the conflicting evidence shown — the system refuses to guess.

Example workflow

How a build usually runs

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Documents arrive from inbox, folder, or API — no new upload habits required.

  2. 02

    Extract

    Fields pulled into your schema, tables assembled.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Rules and confidence scoring check every field.

  4. 04

    Exception queue

    Only flagged items reach a human, with the source opened alongside.

  5. 05

    Push

    Validated data writes to your system of record.

Illustrative flow — the real sequence is set in your written scope.

FAQ

Asked about this system.

What happens when extraction is unsure?

It says so. Confidence flags route uncertain fields to a human queue with the source document opened — uncertainty is surfaced, never averaged away.

Can it run against our historical backlog?

Yes — batch runs are a standard part of the scope, processed in controlled, logged passes.

Does it learn from corrections?

Corrections are captured and fed back into rules and prompts on a review cadence you control.
