Turns your own training material into quizzes and onboarding paths that actually measure whether people understood — with scores, gap reports and audit trails.

What it does

The system, feature by feature.

Content-to-question generation

Questions drafted directly from your material, mapped to a topic taxonomy you approve.

Difficulty and distractor tuning

Plausible wrong answers and calibrated difficulty, reviewed by your subject experts.

Autoscoring with rubrics

Multiple choice scored instantly; open answers graded against rubrics with samples.

Adaptive practice paths

Weak topics trigger targeted drills instead of another lap of everything.

Item-level analytics

See which questions mislead, which topics lag, and which cohorts struggle.

Question bank versioning

Changes tracked over time so retakes and audits stay defensible.

For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written scope.

Architecture

  • Generation. Questions are generated from your material with difficulty labels, and each item's justification anchors to the source section it came from.
  • Scoring logic. Scoring rubrics are explicit and versioned; borderline answers route to human review by default.
  • Data model. Attempt history is stored per person per item, so gap analysis and cohort reporting are queries, not manual work.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Item validity. Generated items are reviewed against the source by a human before they count; ambiguous items are flagged, not guessed.
  • Mastery criteria. Pass thresholds are defined with you before launch and applied consistently across cohorts.

Operations

  • Audit trail. Who took what, when, against which version of the material — recorded for compliance-friendly export.
  • Integrity controls. Shuffled options, timed windows and attempt limits are configuration, not custom work.
  • Failure behavior. A failed save preserves the attempt locally and re-syncs; learners never lose a completed test.

FAQ

Asked about this system.

Does this replace our LMS?

Usually not. It generates and maintains the assessment layer — deployed standalone or connected into your LMS where it supports SCORM or LTI.

How accurate is machine-generated assessment?

The generation is a draft engine. Every question goes through your subject experts before it counts — the workflow is designed around that review.

Can it grade open-ended answers?

Yes, against rubrics you approve, with flagged low-confidence answers routed to a human grader.
