In plain words

A step-by-step production line for written content — research, outline, draft, human review, publish — where AI does the heavy lifting and people approve what ships.

An easy way to picture itThink of a kitchen with stations: prep, cooking, plating — and a head chef who tastes everything before it leaves the pass.

Pipeline
A fixed sequence of steps that content moves through, with checks at each stage.
Human-in-the-loop
A person approves at defined checkpoints — automation does the work, people keep the judgment.
Grounded generation
Writing that must trace back to an approved source; unsupported claims fail the checks.

What it does

The system, feature by feature.

Source-grounded drafting

Every draft cites the research it came from — interviews, docs, data, transcripts.

Outline-first generation

You approve the structure before a word of prose is written.

Your style guide, encoded

Multi-format expansion

Review states and comments

Publishing hooks

Approved pieces push to your CMS or scheduler in the right format.

For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written plan.

Architecture

  • Orchestration. Explicit workflow stages with typed inputs and outputs; retries and timeouts are configured, not improvised.
  • Grounding. Facts and quotes are extracted from your approved sources and carried with citations into the draft; unsupported claims fail automated checks.
  • Publishing. CMS and channel adapters run in dry-run mode first; formatting rules are templates, not manual fixes.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Citation integrity. Every claim in a draft must trace to an approved source before human review — measured, not assumed.
  • Edit distance. How much humans change per draft is tracked per content type: the honest signal for where the pipeline needs tuning.

Operations

  • Stage metrics. Time, cost and rejection rate per stage on a dashboard; anomalies alert a human.
  • Rollback. Published pieces carry their pipeline provenance; corrections propagate downstream consistently.
  • Failure behavior. A failed stage halts that piece with the error attached — never silently publishes degraded output.

FAQ

Asked about this system.

Is this just a ChatGPT wrapper?

No. The value is the pipeline around the model: source grounding, outline gates, an encoded style guide, versioned review, and publishing hooks. The model is a replaceable component inside it.

Who is accountable for what gets published?

Your editors. Nothing ships without a human approval step — that is a design rule for this system, not a settings toggle.

Can it match our voice?

That is exactly what the encoded style guide is for, and it is tuned against your existing published work during the build.
