AI Scope Generator · free, instant

Describe the work. Get a draft plan.

Most studios gate this behind a discovery call. Here it is up front: describe your workflow, answer four quick questions, and receive a draft written plan with a readiness read-out — generated in your browser, in seconds.

Runs entirely on this page Nothing uploaded, nothing stored Draft v0 — a human writes the real one
How to read this

This generator is a transparent heuristic — matched signals are shown, the logic lives in the page source, and it never pretends to be the final word. It exists to save you a discovery call and to show you exactly what a ReWoo plan looks like. The real plan is written by the founder, after a conversation, and it is yours to take anywhere.

The workflow, the pain, and what done looks like. Two or three sentences is plenty — specificity improves the draft.

Why a scope generator

The first touch is a document, not a sales call.

You see the format first

A ReWoo engagement opens with a written plan: objective, what is in and out, milestones, evaluation, and terms. This generator drafts that shape before we ever speak — no gate, no form funnel.

Transparent, not magic

Matched signals are shown as chips, the heuristic lives in the page source, and the output is labeled a draft. An AI tool that explains itself — a small demonstration of how the studio builds.

A better first call

Bring the draft, tear it apart together. The thirty minutes go into your workflow and its edges — not into discovering the basics from zero.

Questions

About the generator.

Is this the final plan?

No — it is a draft to start the conversation. The real plan is written by a human after a short call, and it is yours to keep either way.

Does my description get sent anywhere?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser — there is no server behind this page. Your text leaves your device only if you choose to send the draft by email or WhatsApp yourself.

What if the generator's suggestion is wrong?

Then the first call fixes it — that is what the call is for. The draft is a starting point for a conversation with the person who would actually build the system, and an honest mapping beats a clever guess.

Why does it sometimes recommend an audit instead of a build?

Because that is frequently the right answer. When a description is broad, data looks unready, or no clear signal matches, the honest first step is a readiness audit — not jumping into a build on guesswork. The generator is allowed to say “slow down.”

Prefer a human immediately?

Message WhatsApp and talk to the founder directly — thirty minutes, honest mapping, no sales floor.
