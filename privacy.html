Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · ReWoo, Dhaka, Bangladesh

This Privacy Policy describes how ReWoo ("we", "us", or "our"), based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit rewoo.tech or join our waitlist.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our services:

  • Email address — when you submit the waitlist form on our website, we collect your email address to contact you about ReWoo services.
  • Usage data — anonymised analytics data including pages visited, time spent, and referral source. This data is collected by Plausible Analytics and does not use cookies or track you across websites.
  • Session recordings — Microsoft Clarity may record anonymised session data (mouse movements, clicks, scroll depth) to help us understand how users interact with our site. This data is aggregated and not linked to your identity.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you about ReWoo services when you join the waitlist.
  • To improve our website based on aggregated usage patterns.
  • We will never sell your data to third parties.
  • We will never use your email for newsletters or marketing without your explicit consent.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our website:

  • Plausible Analytics (plausible.io) — Privacy-first analytics. No cookies. No cross-site tracking. Data is aggregated and anonymised. Plausible is GDPR compliant by design.
  • Microsoft Clarity (clarity.microsoft.com) — Session recording and heatmaps. Data is anonymised. See Microsoft's privacy policy for details.
  • Google Fonts — We load fonts from Google's CDN. Google may log your IP address. See Google's privacy policy for details.

4. Data Retention

  • Waitlist email addresses are retained until you request deletion or ReWoo ceases operations.
  • Analytics data is retained in aggregated form indefinitely.
  • Session recording data is retained for the period specified by Microsoft Clarity's default retention policy.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data at any time.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Opt-out — opt out of any future communications from us.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at adilshamim696@gmail.com. We will respond within 5 business days.

6. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. Plausible Analytics is cookieless by design. Microsoft Clarity may use functional cookies for session replay. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. Email addresses collected via the waitlist form are stored securely and access is restricted to authorised ReWoo personnel only.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services evolve. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:
