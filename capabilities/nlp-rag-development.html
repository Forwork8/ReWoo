For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written scope.

Architecture

  • Hybrid retrieval. Lexical and semantic search combined, tuned per corpus — one without the other fails differently.
  • Chunking strategy. Splitting and metadata designed around your document shapes, not a default number.
  • Rerank and cite. Reranking before generation, and citation enforcement after it — answers with receipts.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Retrieval hit rate. Measured separately from answer faithfulness, on the golden question set.
  • Faithfulness scoring. Answers checked against sources by rubric; unsupported statements count against the score.

Operations

  • Index health. Freshness and coverage monitored; stale sources flagged.
  • Permission-aware. Retrieval enforces access control at query time — no answer leaks from a document the asker cannot read.

FAQ

Asked about this capability.

Is RAG already obsolete?

No — it keeps evolving, and the fundamentals (retrieval quality, evaluation, honesty) keep mattering. We build against current best practice and keep the model layer replaceable.

How do you measure quality?

With an evaluation set built from your real questions, scored on retrieval hit quality and answer faithfulness. The report is a deliverable, not an internal artifact.

Can it respect our permissions?

Yes — retrieval is filtered by the access rules of the querying user. Security review of the design is part of handover.
