NLP & RAG Development

The unglamorous foundation everything else stands on: extraction, classification, search, and retrieval over your documents — engineered and evaluated, not prompted and prayed over.

In plain words

Search that answers questions instead of listing links — built on your own documents, always showing where each answer came from.

An easy way to picture itThe archive clerk who has read everything once, then answers with the page number.

Embedding
A numeric fingerprint of a text, used to find passages with similar meaning.
Vector store
The database that holds those fingerprints and searches them fast.
Chunking
How documents are split into searchable pieces — a design decision, not a default.

New to AI systems? Every page on this site opens with one of these summaries — or start with the studio guides, written for beginners.

The problem

The gap this fills.

Retrieval-augmented generation is easy to demo and hard to keep honest. Chunking strategy, hybrid search, reranking, evaluation sets, and no-answer behavior decide whether users trust the system or quietly stop using it. This capability is that engineering, done properly.

For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written plan.

Architecture

  • Hybrid retrieval. Lexical and semantic search combined, tuned per corpus — one without the other fails differently.
  • Chunking strategy. Splitting and metadata designed around your document shapes, not a default number.
  • Rerank and cite. Reranking before generation, and citation enforcement after it — answers with receipts.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Retrieval hit rate. Measured separately from answer faithfulness, on the golden question set.
  • Faithfulness scoring. Answers checked against sources by rubric; unsupported statements count against the score.

Operations

  • Index health. Freshness and coverage monitored; stale sources flagged.
  • Permission-aware. Retrieval enforces access control at query time — no answer leaks from a document the asker cannot read.

Scope

What the engagement covers.

In scope

  • Chunking, embedding, and indexing strategy
  • Hybrid retrieval and reranking
  • Evaluation harness with your real queries
  • No-answer and low-confidence behavior
  • Freshness and re-indexing design
  • Access control over retrieved content

Not included

  • ×Retrieval without evaluation — we do not ship unmeasured
  • ×One corpus to rule them all, ignoring access roles
  • ×Fine-tuning where retrieval quality is the real problem
  • ×Answer generation with no citations

FAQ

Asked about this capability.

Is RAG already obsolete?

No — it keeps evolving, and the fundamentals (retrieval quality, evaluation, honesty) keep mattering. We build against current best practice and keep the model layer replaceable.

How do you measure quality?

With an evaluation set built from your real questions, scored on retrieval hit quality and answer faithfulness. The report is a deliverable, not an internal artifact.

Can it respect our permissions?

Yes — retrieval is filtered by the access rules of the querying user. Security review of the design is part of handover.
