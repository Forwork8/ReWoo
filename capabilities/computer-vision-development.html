For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written scope.

Architecture

  • Task-matched models. Detection, classification or measurement chosen per task, with a measured baseline on your data before promises are made.
  • Deployment placement. Edge versus cloud decided explicitly by latency, privacy and cost — documented either way.
  • Review loop. Low-confidence cases route to humans with the frame attached.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Labeled-set accuracy. Precision and recall on a labeled set of your real images or video, with thresholds agreed in scope.
  • Drift watch. Live performance monitored against the baseline; retraining is a decision, not a surprise.

Operations

  • Flagged-frame archive. Every flagged frame kept with its confidence score for audit.
  • Calibration. Thresholds adjustable per site or shift — one dial, not a rebuild.

FAQ

Asked about this capability.

What accuracy can we expect?

The only honest answer is measured on your data under your conditions — which is exactly what the evaluation deliverable is. Public benchmark numbers are not transferable.

Can it run on our hardware?

Often yes. Edge deployment is a scoping question we answer with measurements on your target device before you commit.

What about privacy?

Scopes exclude people-identification work, and monitoring deployments require a lawful basis and consent design you confirm with your own legal review.
