In plain words

Systems that understand speech or speak back — transcribing calls, answering by voice, or handling voice notes end to end.

An easy way to picture itA switchboard operator from the future: understands accents, takes messages, books things, and knows when to ring a person.

STT / TTS
Speech-to-text and text-to-speech — the ears and the voice.
Latency
The delay in a live voice conversation; the make-or-break number.
Barge-in
Handling interruptions mid-sentence, like a real conversation allows.

New to AI systems? Every page on this site opens with one of these summaries — or start with the studio guides, written for beginners.

The problem

The gap this fills.

Voice is where most AI deployments embarrass themselves: robotic pacing, hallucinated answers, no ability to actually check or book anything, and no clean way out for the caller. A usable voice system is an engineering effort across speech, dialogue, and your real systems — not a phone number wired to a chatbot.

For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written scope.

Architecture

  • Streaming architecture. Streaming speech-to-text and text-to-speech with measured end-to-end latency budgets per turn.
  • Turn-taking. Interruptions and barge-in handled explicitly in the conversation layer.
  • Deterministic backends. Lookups and bookings run on ordinary, testable code behind the voice front-end.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Live measurement. Latency and comprehension tested on real call samples in your languages and accents.
  • Scenario completion. Task completion rate on scripted call scenarios, including the awkward ones.

Operations

  • Per-call traces. Audio, transcript, actions and outcomes linked for every call.
  • Compliance. Consent, retention and redaction configured to your regulatory context.

Scope

What the engagement covers.

In scope

  • Speech-to-text and text-to-speech pipeline
  • Dialogue design with barge-in and repair handling
  • Action interfaces: lookups, bookings, transfers
  • Latency budget engineering end-to-end
  • Consent, retention, and redaction design
  • Call analytics and quality dashboards

Not included

  • ×Replacing your carrier or phone numbers
  • ×Consent and compliance sign-off — that stays with your legal review
  • ×Voice cloning of real people without their documented consent
  • ×Unattended actions on sensitive accounts

FAQ

Asked about this capability.

How natural will it sound?

Modern speech systems are genuinely good — but we set expectations in writing from pilot measurements on your calls, not from demo videos.

What happens when it hits a wall?

It transfers to a human with full context passed along. Handoff is a designed feature, not a failure state.

Can it run in our languages?

Support depends on the language and the speech stack — we validate your specific languages during scoping before you commit.
