In plain words

We help your leadership decide where AI actually pays off — with an honest map and a first plan, not a hype deck.

An easy way to picture itA feasibility study before construction: what to build, what to skip, in what order, and what it will really take.

Opportunity map
A ranked list of where AI could help, by value against effort.
Build vs buy
Whether to build custom or use an existing product — decided per use case.
TCO
Total cost of ownership: the honest lifetime cost, not just the build quote.

New to AI systems? Every page on this site opens with one of these summaries — or start with the studio guides, written for beginners.

The problem

The gap this fills.

Most AI strategy decks are generic maturity models with your logo pasted on. What teams need is narrower and more useful: which two workflows are worth automating first, what the build actually involves, what it does not, and a scope document concrete enough to hand to engineers.

For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written scope.

Architecture

  • Structured discovery. Interviews, system access review and workflow observation — evidence before opinions.
  • Scoring model. Value, feasibility, data readiness and risk scored with weights you agree to beforehand.
  • Explicit no-gos. Recommendations include what not to do and why — the fastest way to save money.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Traceability. Every recommendation traces to an observed workflow and states its assumptions.
  • Review gate. You challenge the map in a working session before it is finalized.

Operations

  • Decision log. Each recommendation keeps its rationale, so later reviews are comparisons, not re-litigations.
  • Deliverables. Written report, walkthrough session, and the scoring model left with you for reuse.

Scope

What the engagement covers.

In scope

  • Workflow discovery sessions with your team
  • Opportunity matrix: value, feasibility, risk
  • Build-vs-buy recommendations
  • Data and integration readiness review
  • Prioritized roadmap with dependencies
  • A scoped first engagement, ready to start

Not included

  • ×Generic maturity-theater slide decks

FAQ

Asked about this capability.

Will you tell us not to use AI?

Sometimes, for specific workflows, yes — and we will write down why. A roadmap that recommends building everything is a sales document, not a strategy.

Do you also implement?

Yes — this is a studio, not a slide shop. But the roadmap stands on its own, and you can take the scope to any builder.

How long is an engagement?

Scope documents set milestones and duration per engagement. We do not promise calendar outcomes in marketing copy.
