In plain words

A chat assistant for your website or app that answers from your real information and hands tricky cases to a human.

An easy way to picture itLike a well-trained receptionist who never sleeps, always answers from the handbook, and knows when to fetch you.

Grounding
Answers are tied to your approved content instead of the model's memory.
Escalation
The defined path that hands a conversation to a human with full context.
Fallback
What the assistant says and does when it is not confident — by design, not by accident.

New to AI systems? Every page on this site opens with one of these summaries — or start with the studio guides, written for beginners.

The problem

The gap this fills.

Most chatbots are decorative: they apologize, deflect, and never read your actual documentation. A useful one is grounded in your content, admits when it does not know, hands off gracefully, and captures what it could not answer so the content gaps get fixed.

For technical evaluators

Under the hood.

Architecture, evaluation and operations — the engineering substance behind the offer. Final choices are set per engagement, in the written scope.

Architecture

  • Grounded responses. Retrieval over your approved content with strict citation; the assistant's knowledge has an address.
  • Conversation engine. An explicit state machine with designed fallback paths — not a prompt hoping for the best.
  • Channels. Web widget, WhatsApp, in-app — one brain, typed channel adapters.

Evaluation & acceptance

  • Golden Q&A set. Answer accuracy measured on real queries from your logs, agreed before build.
  • Escalation review. What got escalated and why, reviewed weekly at launch, until it steadies.

Operations

  • Transcripts. Every conversation stored with outcome labels for audit and improvement.
  • Failure behavior. Low confidence triggers an honest 'I am not sure' plus the human path — never an invented answer.

Scope

What the engagement covers.

In scope

  • Conversation design and tone of voice

  • Citation of sources in answers
  • Escalation and handoff flows

Not included

  • ×Fabricated answers when sources are missing — it says so instead

FAQ

Asked about this capability.

Will it invent answers?

Grounding plus citation plus an explicit no-answer behavior. When the corpus does not contain the answer, it says so and offers the handoff — that is designed, tested behavior.

Can it capture leads?

Yes — with your qualification questions, connected to your CRM, and with consent handled per your privacy policy.

How do we keep it current?

Content changes flow through the pipeline on a refresh cadence set in scope, and the unanswered dashboard shows exactly where content is missing.
