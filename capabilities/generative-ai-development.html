Generative AI Development

Generative systems that hold brand, format, and facts steady — built as pipelines with review gates, not as prompt lotteries.

Scoped engagementFixed quote after discovery
Discuss this project →See the process

The problem

The gap this fills.

Foundation models are commoditized; the engineering is everything around them. Turning generation into a dependable business capability means grounding, conditioning, evaluation, and review flows — so output is consistent, checkable, and actually yours.

Example applications

What teams build with this.

Brand-consistent image systems

Conditioned generation with palette and style locks.

Documented text generation

Drafts tied to your sources with citations.

Audio and narration pipelines

Voiceovers and audio versions from written content.

Video assembly pipelines

Script, visuals, and cutdowns from one source piece.

Data-to-narrative systems

Reports and summaries generated from structured data.

Synthetic training material

Examples and exercises generated for practice systems.

Scope

What the engagement covers.

In scope

  • Model selection and cost engineering
  • Conditioning: style, format, and brand rules
  • Grounding and citation where facts matter
  • Evaluation sets for output quality
  • Human review and approval flows
  • Batch and interactive deployment modes

Not included

  • ×Deepfake or impersonation work of real people
  • ×Generation without provenance logging
  • ×Undisclosed synthetic content for deception
  • ×Fine-tuning where prompting and conditioning suffice

Inputs and deliverables

What you bring. What you get.

What we need from you

  • Reference content and style examples
  • Volume and format requirements
  • Quality bar examples: good versus unacceptable
  • Where outputs will be used and by whom

Deliverables

  • Deployed generation pipeline
  • Conditioning and rule configuration
  • Evaluation results and quality report
  • Review and approval workflow
  • Cost-per-output measurements
  • Documentation and handover

FAQ

Asked about this capability.

Do you fine-tune custom models?

Only when conditioning and retrieval cannot reach the quality bar. Fine-tuning adds cost and maintenance; we justify it in writing or we do not do it.

How do we control quality at volume?

Rule-based checks, evaluation scoring, and sampled human review — configured to your tolerance, with the logs to prove it.

What does generation cost to run?

We measure cost-per-output during the build and hand you the profile. Model prices change; your pipeline makes the trade-offs visible.
