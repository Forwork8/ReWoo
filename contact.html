    Quick answers.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Will I talk to a salesperson or an engineer?
    An engineer. ReWoo is a studio, not a sales organization — the person who answers is the person who would scope and build.
    
        
    What should I prepare for the call?
    One workflow that hurts and, if you have it, an example of done-right. That is genuinely enough for a first conversation.
    
        
    Do you sign NDAs?
    Yes, routinely — say the word and we will use yours or provide a standard one before anything sensitive is discussed.
    
        
    What happens after the call?
    If there is a fit, you receive a written scope: deliverables, boundaries, milestones, and a fixed quote. Approving it is entirely your decision, in your time.
