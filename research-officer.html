How it works

01
We parse your notebook
We extract every code cell, output, metric, and markdown note — building a structured picture of your research.
02

Detected methods (LightGBM, SHAP, sklearn…) are matched to real arXiv papers — no invented references, ever.
03

Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion — written by AI with your exact metrics, streamed live as you watch.
04

05
