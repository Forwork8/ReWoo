Industry

AI systems for Real Estate & PropTech

Listing production, inquiry handling, and portfolio document operations.

Discuss a real estate workflow →
Note

These are possible workflows, not a client list — a discovery aid for mapping ReWoo's systems and capabilities onto your operation.

Context

Where the pressure sits.

Listings are visual products on a deadline, inquiries arrive at all hours, and transactions generate document piles measured in reams. All three are systematizable without losing the personal touch that closes deals.

Possible workflows

Three places systems typically fit.

Listing visual production

  1. Property photos and brand rules in
  2. Variations and staging concepts generated
  3. Agent review and selection
  4. Portal-ready exports

Builds on Visual Production Systems and Design Visualization.

Inquiry qualification

  1. Chat and voice assistants grounded in listing data
  2. Qualification questions asked politely
  3. Viewings booked to agent calendars

Builds on chatbot and voice capabilities.

Transaction document flow

  1. Contracts and forms ingested
  2. Key dates and fields extracted
  3. Human exception queue
  4. CRM and file updated

Builds on Document Intelligence.

Relevant systems

Solution families that fit.

Visual Production Systems

Brand-safe image generation pipelines that turn one brief into a full set of on-brand visuals.

Design Visualization

Sketches, specs, and floor plans turned into realistic visuals your team and clients can react to.

Document Intelligence

Extraction, classification, and cross-checking for document-heavy operations — invoices, applications, contracts, claims.

Considerations

Generated staging visuals are labeled as such — misleading property imagery is a line we do not cross, and listing-portal rules on AI imagery are configured per platform.
