Industry

AI systems for Hospitality & Travel

Booking flows, guest communication, and review intelligence across languages.

Discuss a hospitality workflow →
Note

These are possible workflows, not a client list — a discovery aid for mapping ReWoo's systems and capabilities onto your operation.

Context

Where the pressure sits.

Guests write at midnight in six languages about bookings made on three platforms. Hospitality runs on responsiveness — and responsiveness is exactly what grounded assistants and voice systems do well, with a clean handoff to your staff when judgment is needed.

Possible workflows

Three places systems typically fit.

Multilingual guest assistance

  1. Property knowledge grounded
  2. Guest questions answered in their language
  3. Human handoff with context

Builds on AI Chatbot Development.

Booking-line voice handling

  1. Calls answered and transcribed
  2. Availability checked live
  3. Reservations confirmed or escalated

Builds on Voice & Communication Intelligence.

Review and feedback intelligence

  1. Reviews aggregated and classified
  2. Themes and recurring issues surfaced
  3. Response drafts for approval

Builds on NLP & RAG development.

Relevant systems

Solution families that fit.

Voice & Communication Intelligence

Transcription, summarization, and routing for calls and voice notes — with an optional voice agent on top.

Knowledge Search & Retrieval

Ask your company's documents questions — answers with citations you can click and check.

Considerations

Tone is the product in hospitality — assistants are tuned on your voice examples, and anything affecting reservations escalates to staff. Channel rules (WhatsApp, OTA messaging) are handled per platform policy.
