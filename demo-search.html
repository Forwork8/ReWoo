Live demo · 01

Knowledge search, with receipts.

Ask this studio's own knowledge base a question. Every answer shows the passage it came from — click the citation and check us. Then re-run the retrieval benchmark yourself, failures included.

Runs in your browser No signup Retrieval only — no generation
What this is

A real BM25 search engine over 34 passages of ReWoo's own published content — the guides, the process, the systems, the FAQs. It runs entirely on this page: no server, no API, nothing leaves your device. It retrieves passages and shows citations; it does not generate prose, so it cannot hallucinate.

Try it

Ask the knowledge base.

No results yet — ask a question above, or tap a sample.

Every result carries its citation. In a client build the same discipline applies to your documents: no citation, no answer.

Under the hood

How this demo works.

The entire engine is ~90 lines of vanilla JavaScript you can read in this page's source.

The corpus

34 passages from ReWoo's own site — three guides, the five-step process, seven system families, five engagements, and the honest answers. Real content, no filler.

The ranker

Classic BM25 (k1 = 1.2, b = 0.75) with English and Bangla tokenization and a 27-term stop-word list. No embeddings, no reranker — deliberately simple, so the benchmark is meaningful.

The behavior

Passages below a zero score are not shown — the honest empty state. In a client build that becomes a proper “no confident answer” with escalation, instead of a guess.

Published benchmark

Re-run our retrieval benchmark.

19 questions with known gold passages, scored live in your browser at the moment you press the button. Method and failures in the open — the studio's evaluation habit, applied to itself.

BM25 · 34 passages · 19 questions

Metrics: Hit@1, Hit@3, MRR, and the misses. The gold passage for each question was fixed before any tuning — a question counts as answered only if its gold passage ranks first.

QuestionGold passageRank

Why publish a benchmark on a marketing site? Because “we evaluate our systems” is a claim, and this page is the check. The same discipline — fixed gold sets, measured results, failures listed — ships with every client build, scored against your own questions instead of ours.

From demo to system

What a client build adds.

Your corpus, your rules

Hybrid retrieval (keyword + semantic) over your policies, specs, and manuals — with role-aware access so people only see sources they may see.

Citation-first answers

Generated answers stay grounded: every claim links to the passage it came from, and anything unsupported is refused rather than improvised.

Evaluation on your questions

A golden set of your real questions is collected first; acceptance thresholds are agreed in writing — and the failure report is part of delivery.
