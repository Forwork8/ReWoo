About

An AI systems studio, built like the work it ships.

Small, senior, and deliberately narrow: we scope, design, and build AI systems — then hand them over completely.

Independent studio Dhaka, Bangladesh Working with teams anywhere

The studio

Started in Dhaka. Built for anywhere.

ReWoo started in Dhaka, Bangladesh — founded by a builder with a first-author research paper, a Kaggle Master rank, and a first client in the United Kingdom. The studio's origin is its method: work that survives scrutiny, delivered from a place global players do not reach.

Today ReWoo designs and builds AI systems for teams at every stage — from a founder automating their first workflow to enterprises with governance requirements. What has not changed is the shape of the work: a written plan, a system built around the client's own stack, and a complete handover.

The studio stays deliberately small. The person who scopes your system is the person who builds it, evaluates it, and hands it over. That constraint is a feature: it keeps every claim on this site checkable, because the person making it signs the work.

The founder

The person who scopes it is the person who builds it.

ReWoo is run by Adil, an AI systems engineer from Dhaka, Bangladesh. Before the studio there was research: a first-author publication on Bengali speaker diarization — the problem of deciding who spoke when in audio — and a Kaggle Master rank earned in open competition. Both taught the same lesson the studio now sells: claims should be measured, evaluated, and falsifiable.

The studio's first client engagement was delivered for a team in the United Kingdom, and the shape of that work set the template every engagement still follows: a written plan before anything began, a system built around the client's own stack, and a complete handover.

Staying deliberately small is the design, not a stage. One mind carries your system from first call to handover — no account managers, no hand-offs, no telephone game between the person who promised and the person who builds.

How we work

Five commitments, in writing.

Plan before build

No work begins without a written plan: what gets built, the steps, the timeline, and every term. Approved by you, or it does not happen.

Own your system

Code, configuration, credentials, and documentation transfer at handover. Lock-in is a business model we decline.

Evaluate, then say it straight

Systems are scored against your real cases, failures included. If the bar cannot be met, you get a stop recommendation, not a soft landing.

Design for the boring days

Retries, idempotency, logs, alerts, runbooks, and human exception queues — the unglamorous engineering that keeps systems alive after the demo.

Say no when no is the answer

Some workflows should not be automated, some data is not ready, some budgets are better unspent. An honest no is part of the service.

This site, by these rules

Every offer on this website is labeled as built, proposed, or exploratory — the studio runs its own storefront by the standards it sells.

What the studio carries into every build

Research-grade habits, production-grade results.

The founder background — a first-author research publication and a Kaggle Master ranking — shows up as discipline, not decoration: evaluation sets instead of anecdotes, measured claims instead of marketing numbers, and experiments designed to be falsifiable.

That is also why this website states plainly which parts of ReWoo's offering are built, which are proposed engagements, and which are explorations for the future. A studio that sells rigor has to run its own site by the same rules.
