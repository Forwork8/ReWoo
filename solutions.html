Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions.
Clear Answers for Operators.

Do I or my team need technical or coding skills?

Zero technical effort is required from your side. You explain your workflow, goals, and existing software in plain English. ReWoo engineers the entire system, integrates your APIs, stress-tests against edge cases, and provides a simple 1-click or fully autonomous experience.

Who owns the code, prompts, and Intellectual Property?

You own 100% of all code, prompt architectures, database connectors, and IP. There is zero vendor lock-in. Once built, the system is deployed into your accounts or infrastructure and you retain total control.

How do you prevent the AI from making mistakes or hallucinating?

We do not rely on standard open-ended prompts. Every system employs deterministic validators, schema checks, retrieval-augmented grounding against verified documents, and human-in-the-loop escalation triggers for low-confidence scenarios.

How long does it take from our first call to a live system?

Most standard agent systems (voice receptionists, outbound SDRs, data pipelines, document OCR extraction) are delivered and running in your business within 2 to 5 business days. Complex enterprise custom systems with multiple internal databases typically take 5 to 8 business days.

What happens if one of our connected tools changes or updates its API?

Our autonomous retainers include 24/7 uptime monitoring, error alerting, and proactive maintenance for upstream API updates. We handle any breaking changes immediately so your business never experiences operational downtime.
