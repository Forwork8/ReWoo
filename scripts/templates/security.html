ReWoo is an independent studio, not an ISO-certified enterprise. This page describes exactly how data is handled today — including the limits — because a studio selling honest systems runs its own claims the same way. Formal certification is on the roadmap, not on this page.

The rules

Your code. Your data. Your keys.

Systems live in your accounts

Builds deploy into infrastructure you own — your cloud account, your repositories, your API keys. Nothing production-critical lives in ReWoo's hands after handover.

Least access, time-boxed

During a build, access is scoped to what the milestone needs and revoked at handover. Credentials rotate on delivery. Access is never broader than the written scope.

No silent retention

After handover, ReWoo retains no client data unless support is explicitly scoped. Working copies are deleted; what remains is the engagement record you also hold.

In detail

The handling, stage by stage.

Discovery

NDAs signed before sensitive material is shared — yours or a standard mutual one. What you share before an NDA is treated as confidential anyway, contractually.

Building

Test data stays inside the environment agreed in the scope. Redaction and synthetic substitution are offered before real customer data ever touches a prototype.

Model calls

Which providers may receive which data is an explicit scope line. For data that cannot leave your infrastructure, private or on-premise delivery with open-weight models is a scoped option.

Consent design

Systems that touch people — callers, candidates, customers — ship with consent and disclosure flows designed in, not bolted on. Recording notices and opt-outs are part of the build.

Logs and traces

Observability is designed for privacy: logs carry what the runbook needs, retention windows are set in the scope, and sensitive fields are masked at write time, not by promise.

Handover

Everything transfers — repository, configuration, credentials (rotated), documentation, runbooks. ReWoo's access ends when you say the engagement is done.

Deployment

Three places a system can live.

Your cloud

The default: the system deploys into your AWS, GCP, Azure, or on-prem environment. ReWoo builds inside it; ownership never leaves it.

Private / on-premise

For banks, healthcare, and government-adjacent work: open-weight models and local retrieval on hardware you control, with no external API calls. Scoped per engagement.

Hybrid

Sensitive data stays local; only non-sensitive steps use external models. The split is drawn in the written scope and enforced in the routing layer.

For procurement

What you can ask for, and receive.

  • A mutual NDA — yours or ours — signed before anything sensitive moves
  • IP assignment and confidentiality terms written into the engagement, not assumed
  • A data-processing description per engagement: what is touched, where it lives, how long it stays
  • Security documentation of the design, ready for your review team
  • Phased rollouts with checkpoints as the default shape for larger organizations
  • Complete handover at the end — no recurring dependency unless you scope it
