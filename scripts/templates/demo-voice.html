      In production

      What a real system measures.

      Every voice system ships with its evaluation — the numbers below are the ones agreed in writing before any build is accepted.

      Word error rate

      Transcription accuracy scored on a sample of your real recordings — your accents, your audio quality, your vocabulary. Bangla and code-switched speech measured separately.

      Diarization error rate

      Speaker-separation quality on your calls — the metric the founder's research addresses directly for Bengali audio, where general tools are weakest.

      Latency, end to end

      Round-trip from speech to response in your actual telephony stack — because for voice, the difference between natural and unbearable is measured in hundreds of milliseconds.

      Escalation accuracy

      How often the system correctly routes to a human — and how often it escalates unnecessarily. Both directions are reported, per intent.

      Review queue health

      What lands in the human exception queue, how fast it clears, and what it teaches: corrections feed back into the evaluation set.

      Failure log, included

      The calls that went wrong ship in the report. If the bar cannot be met on your audio, you get a stop recommendation — before you spend the full build.
