Questions

Asked often.

Do you run ready-made AI products we can subscribe to?

Not yet — and the order of operations is deliberate. ReWoo builds scoped systems to order, and productizes what the work proves out: the AI Scope Generator and the live demos on this site are the studio's own product work, built in public. When an exploration earns it, it ships with the same evaluation and handover discipline as a client build. Products generate evidence — that is the direction.

How do you price?

Indicative durations and price ranges are published on the engagements page — labeled estimates, so you can filter by budget before any call. The final number is always a fixed quote inside a written scope, set after discovery, and nothing is charged before you approve that document.

What if the build is not feasible?

You learn it early and in writing. Milestone structure and evaluation reports are designed to surface failure before it becomes expensive — including a stop recommendation when the bar cannot be met.

Who owns the code and data?

You do. Handover transfers the repository, configuration, credentials, and documentation. There is no hostage-taking and no recurring dependency unless you explicitly scope support.

Where is the team based?

Dhaka, Bangladesh — an independent studio working with teams anywhere. The current site you are reading, including its live demos and the retrieval benchmark, was designed and built in-house.

Can you work with our security and procurement process?

Yes — designs are documented for security review, scopes are written to survive procurement, and phased rollouts with checkpoints are the default shape for larger organizations. The security and data-handling page lists where data lives and how it is treated.
