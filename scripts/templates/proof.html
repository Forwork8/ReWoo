Case study

The first client: United Kingdom.

In preparation — pending client sign-off

The studio's first engagement was delivered for a team in the United Kingdom: a scoped system built around their stack, with a written scope before anything was charged and a complete handover after. The full case study publishes with the client's permission — measured results, what worked, and what we would do differently. No numbers appear here until they are real and approved.

What will be published

  1. The problem. The workflow as it ran before — volumes, bottlenecks, and the decision the system feeds.
  2. The scope. Deliverables, boundaries, milestones, and the fixed quote as written.
  3. The build. Stack, integration points, and the evaluation set built from the client's real cases.
  4. The measured outcome. Before-and-after numbers on accuracy, hours, and quality — with the failures and near-misses included.
  5. The handover. What the client received and runs today, in their own words if they offer them.

Until sign-off, the verifiable facts stand as stated: the engagement existed, it followed the written-scope method, and it completed with full handover. Reference available on request for qualified inquiries.
