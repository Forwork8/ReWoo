Under the hood

How this demo works.

The entire engine is ~90 lines of vanilla JavaScript you can read in this page's source.

The corpus

34 passages from ReWoo's own site — three guides, the five-step process, seven system families, five engagements, and the honest answers. Real content, no filler.

The ranker

Classic BM25 (k1 = 1.2, b = 0.75) with English and Bangla tokenization and a 27-term stop-word list. No embeddings, no reranker — deliberately simple, so the benchmark is meaningful.

The behavior

Passages below a zero score are not shown — the honest empty state. In a client build that becomes a proper “no confident answer” with escalation, instead of a guess.

Published benchmark

Re-run our retrieval benchmark.

19 questions with known gold passages, scored live in your browser at the moment you press the button. Method and failures in the open — the studio's evaluation habit, applied to itself.

From demo to system

What a client build adds.

Your corpus, your rules

Hybrid retrieval (keyword + semantic) over your policies, specs, and manuals — with role-aware access so people only see sources they may see.

Citation-first answers

Generated answers stay grounded: every claim links to the passage it came from, and anything unsupported is refused rather than improvised.

Evaluation on your questions

A golden set of your real questions is collected first; acceptance thresholds are agreed in writing — and the failure report is part of delivery.
